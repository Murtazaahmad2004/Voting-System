Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

01Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and any details you share when contacting support. We also automatically collect device information, app usage data, and approximate location to improve reliability.

02How we use your data

  • To provide and maintain core app functionality
  • To respond to support requests and account questions
  • To send important service updates and security notices
  • To improve performance and detect misuse

03Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate the app, such as hosting and analytics partners, under strict confidentiality obligations.

04Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time, and we will remove your data within 30 days except where retention is required by law.

05Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.
